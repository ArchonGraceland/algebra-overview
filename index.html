01Order of Operations (PEMDAS)

Parentheses → Exponents → Multiplication & Division (left to right) → Addition & Subtraction (left to right)

Example: \( 2 + 3 \times (4 - 1)^2 = 29 \)

02Negative Number Rules

Sign Rules for Multiplication / Division

  • Same signs → Positive
  • Different signs → Negative
  • \( (-4) \times (-6) = 24 \)
  • \( 12 \div (-3) = -4 \)

Addition / Subtraction

\( a + (-b) = a - b \)

\( a - (-b) = a + b \)

03Properties of Real Numbers

Commutative: \( a + b = b + a \), \( a \times b = b \times a \)
Associative: \( (a + b) + c = a + (b + c) \)
Distributive: \( a(b + c) = ab + ac \)
Identity: \( a + 0 = a \), \( a \times 1 = a \)
Inverse: \( a + (-a) = 0 \), \( a \times \frac{1}{a} = 1 \) (a ≠ 0)

04Exponent Rules

\( a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n} \)
\( \frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n} \) (a ≠ 0)
\( (a^m)^n = a^{m \cdot n} \)
\( a^0 = 1 \) (a ≠ 0)
\( a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n} \) (a ≠ 0)
\( (ab)^n = a^n \cdot b^n \)

05Fraction Rules

  • Multiply: \( \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a \cdot c}{b \cdot d} \)
  • Divide: \( \frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a \cdot d}{b \cdot c} \)
  • Add/Subtract: Common denominator → \( \frac{a}{b} + \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ad + bc}{bd} \)

06Solving Equations & Inequalities

Do the same operation to both sides to keep the equation balanced.

Inequality note: When multiplying or dividing by a negative number, flip the inequality sign.

Example: \( -2x > 8 \) → divide by -2 → \( x < -4 \)

07FOIL Method

Multiply two binomials: \( (a + b)(c + d) = ac + ad + bc + bd \)

Example: \( (x + 3)(x - 2) = x^2 + x - 6 \)

08Advanced Factoring

Difference of Squares: \( a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b) \)
Sum of Cubes: \( a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 - ab + b^2) \)
Difference of Cubes: \( a^3 - b^3 = (a - b)(a^2 + ab + b^2) \)
Perfect Square Trinomial: \( a^2 \pm 2ab + b^2 = (a \pm b)^2 \)

09Radical Rules

\( \sqrt[n]{ab} = \sqrt[n]{a} \cdot \sqrt[n]{b} \)
\( \sqrt[n]{\frac{a}{b}} = \frac{\sqrt[n]{a}}{\sqrt[n]{b}} \) (b ≠ 0)
\( \sqrt[n]{a^m} = a^{m/n} \)
Rationalize denominator when needed

10Quadratic Formula

For \( ax^2 + bx + c = 0 \):

\( x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \)

11Complex Numbers

\( i = \sqrt{-1} \), so \( i^2 = -1 \)

Powers of \( i \) cycle every 4: \( i, -1, -i, 1 \)

Multiply using FOIL and replace \( i^2 \) with -1.

12Logarithm Properties

Product: \( \log_b (xy) = \log_b x + \log_b y \)
Quotient: \( \log_b \left( \frac{x}{y} \right) = \log_b x - \log_b y \)
Power: \( \log_b (x^k) = k \log_b x \)
Inverse: \( b^{\log_b x} = x \)

13Function Transformations

For any function \( f(x) \):

  • Up/down: \( f(x) \pm k \)
  • Left/right: \( f(x \pm h) \)
  • Reflection x-axis: \( -f(x) \)
  • Reflection y-axis: \( f(-x) \)
  • Vertical stretch: \( a \cdot f(x) \) (|a| > 1 = stretch)

14Conic Sections (Basic Forms)

Circle: \( (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2 \)
Parabola: \( y = a(x - h)^2 + k \)
Ellipse: \( \dfrac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1 \)
Hyperbola: \( \dfrac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} - \dfrac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1 \)
